Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Trailfit app ("the app") collects and how it is used. The data controller is Maciej Skupień, contact: megii.mb@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Account data: e-mail address and a user identifier created when you sign up (by e-mail or with Google).
  • Profile data: age, gender, weight, height, fitness level, goal, training days per week, session length and time zone.
  • Training data: completed workouts, exercises, sets, results, ratings, workout duration, streaks, points and badges.
  • Leaderboard data: the nickname you choose, your league and monthly points. Your nickname and points are visible to other users.
  • Advertising data: if you use the free version, Google AdMob may process your device's advertising ID and basic device information to show ads.

Health Connect (optional)

If you turn on Health Connect in Settings, the app saves each completed workout (type, start and end time) and its estimated active calories to Health Connect on your phone, so they appear in your health app (e.g. Google Fit, Samsung Health). The app only writes this data and does not read anything from Health Connect. The data stays on your device under Health Connect's control and is not sent to our servers or used for ads. You can turn it off anytime in Settings or in Health Connect.

Sharing cards

When you share a card (after a workout, with a badge, a league promotion or a month summary), the image is created on your phone and is not sent to our servers. Your nickname on the card is optional. The image only goes where you send or save it yourself (e.g. Instagram, Messenger, your gallery) – that service's rules apply there. The QR code on the card links to Trailfit on Google Play.

Why we use it

  • Profile and training data may be data concerning health. We process it based on your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give in the app before your profile is created, to create your training plan and track your progress. We record when you gave consent. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) – we then delete this data. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.
  • Account and leaderboard data – to run your account, leagues and badges (performance of the contract with you).
  • To show ads in the free version (based on your consent where required).
  • To keep the service secure and fix errors (legitimate interest).

We do not sell your data. Health and fitness data is used only to provide the app's features.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Amazon Web Services (Cognito, DynamoDB, Lambda) in the EU (Frankfurt, eu-central-1). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Third parties

  • Amazon Web Services – hosting and sign-in.
  • Google – Google sign-in, Google AdMob (ads and consent), Google Play (app downloads and updates).

You can change your ad consent anytime in the app: Settings → Ad privacy options.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is deleted from our servers.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can request access to, correction, deletion or a copy of your data, restrict or object to processing and withdraw consent. Write to megii.mb@gmail.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Poland: UODO).

Children

The app is not intended for children under 16.

Changes

We will publish any changes on this page and update the date above.
