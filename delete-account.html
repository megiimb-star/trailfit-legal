Delete your account

Last updated: 23 October 2026

You can delete your Trailfit account and all associated data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Trailfit and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If asked, sign in again first.

By e-mail

If you can't use the app, send a request from the e-mail address of your account to megii.mb@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Trailfit account". We will delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, training history, plan, leaderboard entry, points and badges are permanently deleted from our servers. This cannot be undone.

An active Google Play subscription is not cancelled automatically – cancel it in Google Play → Subscriptions.
